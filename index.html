About the park

A 1.6-acre stage on the lake, since 1936.

Gyro Park sits where Main Street meets Lakeshore Drive, anchored by the historic 1951 Gyro Bandshell — a Penticton landmark that's hosted live entertainment since 1936. Grass for a blanket, shade for a picnic, and the lake right there.

Every Thursday from June through mid-September, that same lawn fills with food trucks, local vendors, buskers and neighbours — free to wander, dog and kids welcome.
